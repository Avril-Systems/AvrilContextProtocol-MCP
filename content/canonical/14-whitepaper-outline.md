# Avril Whitepaper — Working Outline

**Status:** THESIS / DRAFT STRUCTURE  
**Scope:** Future public whitepaper  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

The Avril whitepaper should be substantially simpler than the MotusDAO whitepaper.

Target length: approximately 12–18 pages.

## 1. The Transition

Intelligence and operational capability are becoming software.

## 2. The Economic Problem

Human income is still strongly tied to human labor.

Automation may reduce the amount of labor companies require.

## 3. The Paradox

The same technology that automates work may also reduce the resources required to create a productive organization.

## 4. The Avril Thesis

> If agents can perform more of the work of a company, more people should be able to own a company.

## 5. Economic Agency

The human role shifts from performing every task toward:

- ownership
- intention
- judgment
- capital allocation
- approval
- accountability

## 6. The Agentic Company

Define the organization as:

Human owner  
→ Company  
→ Agents  
→ Tools / Context / Infrastructure  
→ Market

## 7. The Product Path

Current:
- Agent Launcher

Emerging:
- preconfigured business agents

Long term:
- Agentic Company OS

## 8. The Infrastructure Stack

Explain without overcommitting:

- runtime
- inference
- context
- tools / MCP
- identity
- permissions
- payments
- monitoring
- human approval

## 9. Interdependence

Agentic companies can become both consumers and providers of software services.

The network may emerge from real company dependencies rather than from a marketplace designed in advance.

## 10. Machine Commerce

Explain the possible role of:

- HTTP-native services
- paywalled APIs
- MCP
- x402
- stablecoins
- machine procurement

## 11. The Human

The final human role is not merely to fund API usage.

The human remains the economic principal.

## 12. Avril Today

Be explicit:

- what exists
- what is WIP
- what is thesis
- what has not been built

## 13. Research Agenda

List the open questions instead of presenting them as solved.

## Tone

The whitepaper should be:

- ambitious
- simple
- falsifiable
- grounded
- technically literate
- explicit about uncertainty

Avoid presenting speculative infrastructure as already necessary.
