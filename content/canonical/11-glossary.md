# Avril Glossary

**Status:** CURRENT  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

## Agent
A persistent or semi-persistent software actor capable of reasoning, using tools, taking actions, and maintaining state.

## Agent Launcher
Avril's current product wedge for deploying and hosting persistent agent environments.

## Avril Lab
Builder-facing hosted agent environment associated with the launcher.

## Agentic Company
A human-owned organization whose operational capacity is substantially executed by AI agents and software services.

## Agentic Company OS
Avril's long-term product thesis: a control plane for creating and operating agentic companies.

## Economic Agency
A person's ability to create, own, direct, exchange, and capture economic value.

## Economic Principal
The human or legal entity that owns, governs, funds, or bears responsibility for an agentic company.

## Human-in-the-loop (HITL)
A workflow where a human approval, judgment, identity, or physical/legal action is required before the system can proceed.

## Capability
A service that an agent can consume, such as inference, context, data, browser execution, search, or a human action.

## MCP
A standard interface used to expose tools and capabilities to agents.

## Context Infrastructure
Systems responsible for deciding what information an agent receives and how that information is retrieved, permissioned, packed, and kept current.

## x402
A machine-payment pattern based on HTTP payment requirements, useful for per-call or programmatic service access.

## Settlement Layer
The payment network or mechanism used to settle machine-to-machine transactions.

## Wedge
A deliberately narrow first product that enters a larger market.

## Vibe Founding
A possible user experience for creating a company through conversational or assisted interfaces. It is not the company-level definition of Avril.
