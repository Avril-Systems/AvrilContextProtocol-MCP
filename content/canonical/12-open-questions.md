# Open Questions

**Status:** OPEN  
**Scope:** Research agenda  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

These questions should remain unresolved until research or real usage provides evidence.

## Company identity

- Is "infrastructure for human-owned agentic companies" the strongest company-level definition?
- Should "economic agency" appear publicly or remain part of the internal thesis?
- How much of the social/economic thesis belongs in investor-facing documentation?

## Launcher

- Who is the first high-retention customer segment?
- Is the buyer a builder, freelancer, SMB, agency, or enterprise team?
- Is the durable unit "agent hosting" or "preconfigured business worker"?
- Which configurations create repeat usage?

## Company OS

- Which company-level problems appear consistently after one agent is deployed?
- When do users need multiple agents?
- Which workflows justify a dedicated control plane?
- Which parts should Avril own vs integrate?

## Human role

- Which tasks consistently require human identity or judgment?
- Can human-required actions be exposed as a capability layer?
- How much autonomy do customers actually want?

## Agent economy

- Which external capabilities do agents repeatedly purchase?
- Which services are naturally priced per request?
- Which services fit HTTP/x402?
- Which services fit MCP paywalls?
- Does an inter-company machine economy emerge organically from real deployments?

## Context

- Is shared company context a universal enough problem to become core infrastructure?
- Should RootRouter remain independent infrastructure, integrate deeply, or simply inform architecture?

## Payments

- Is Base + stablecoin + x402 sufficient as a default settlement path?
- Do customers actually need machine-to-machine payments in the first product?
- At what point does wallet/budget management become a Company OS requirement?

## Fundraising

- What traction threshold is enough to tell the larger Agentic Company OS story credibly?
- Which investor category best matches Avril: AI infrastructure, future of work, enterprise agents, SMB software, or agent economy?
