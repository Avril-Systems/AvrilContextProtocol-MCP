# The Agentic Company Thesis

**Status:** THESIS  
**Scope:** Long-term company thesis  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

## The transition

Modern companies coordinate:

- labor
- capital
- knowledge
- software
- distribution
- decision-making

Historically, creating a company required assembling many of these resources manually.

AI agents change that structure because part of the labor and coordination layer can increasingly become software.

## The paradox

AI may reduce the amount of human labor required by companies.

The same technology may also reduce the amount of capital and organizational complexity required to create one.

That creates the central Avril thesis:

> The technology that automates jobs can also democratize productive ownership.

## Productive ownership

Avril uses the concept of **economic agency** to describe a person's ability to:

- create value
- own productive systems
- direct capital
- make decisions
- participate in markets
- capture part of the economic value generated by those systems

The goal is not to preserve every existing task for humans.

The goal is to preserve and expand human participation in the economy through ownership, direction, and judgment.

## Agentic company

An agentic company is:

> A human-owned organization whose operational capacity is substantially executed by persistent AI agents and software services.

It may contain:

- one human owner
- a small human team
- multiple agents
- specialized external services
- machine-readable tools
- company context
- financial accounts
- approval boundaries
- revenue-generating products or services

## Human-owned

"Human-owned" is important.

Avril does not assume that autonomy itself is the final objective.

The human remains the economic principal.

The agents are productive actors within a system ultimately owned and governed by people or legal entities.

## Simple formulation

**Personal agents:**  
Human → Agent

**Agentic company:**  
Human owner → Company → Agents → Tools / Context / Infrastructure → Market

Avril's unit of design is therefore the organization, not just the individual agent.
