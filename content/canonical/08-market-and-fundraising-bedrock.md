# Market and Fundraising Bedrock

**Status:** CURRENT / RESEARCH  
**Scope:** Company strategy  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

## Principle 1 — Build before fundraising

The company should not rely on the pitch alone.

A simple working product with real users creates leverage.

The target sequence is:

build  
→ get users  
→ show value  
→ raise when useful

The early product does not need to look impressive.

It needs to work.

## Principle 2 — Convince, do not impress

The company narrative should use plain language.

Investors should be able to understand:

- what Avril builds
- who needs it
- what is already working
- why the market may become very large
- why Avril has a credible entry point

## Principle 3 — Product and GTM can have different breadth

A useful pattern for Avril:

**broad product thesis + narrow initial go-to-market**

Long-term product:
- infrastructure / OS for agentic companies

Initial GTM:
- one concrete launcher
- one buyer profile
- one repeatable use case
- one clear commercial offer

## Principle 4 — Infrastructure and applications are both fundable

Current AI funding is concentrated in two broad categories:

### Infrastructure
- inference
- deployment
- cost reduction
- governance
- security
- data
- agent tooling

### Applications
- vertical AI
- enterprise workflows
- industry automation
- revenue-generating outcomes

Avril can plausibly sit between these:

- current wedge: deployment infrastructure
- long-term thesis: company-level application / control plane

## Principle 5 — Investors look for production usage

Important early signals include:

- real users
- paid deployments
- usage growth
- expansion within customers
- recurring consumption
- active pipeline
- evidence that usage is production, not a fake pilot

## Principle 6 — Why now / why us / differentiation

Avril's fundraising story should eventually answer:

### Why now?
Persistent agents are moving from demos into production.

### Why Avril?
Avril is deploying real agents and using those deployments to learn the missing company-level primitives.

### Differentiation
Avril is not trying to win the consumer personal-agent category.

It is focused on the organizational layer: deploying and eventually operating agentic companies.

## Current fundraising hypothesis

A fundable story does not require building the full Company OS before raising.

A credible path can be:

Agent Launcher  
+ real deployments  
+ usage / revenue  
+ recurring company-level problems  
+ clear expansion thesis

The thesis creates the size of the opportunity.

The launcher creates evidence.
