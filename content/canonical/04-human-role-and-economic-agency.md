# The Human Role

**Status:** THESIS  
**Scope:** Economic model / philosophy  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

## The central question

If agents perform more operational work, what is the final role of the human?

Avril should not answer this with "the human pays the API bill."

That may be true during bootstrapping, but it is not a sufficient long-term role.

## The human as principal

The human is the **economic principal**.

The human can provide:

- purpose
- ownership
- initial capital
- strategic judgment
- values
- risk tolerance
- approval
- accountability
- profit allocation
- legal identity where required

Agents provide:

- execution
- research
- repetition
- coordination
- monitoring
- production
- tool use
- operational planning

## Human-required actions

Some workflows may still require humans because of:

- KYC
- legal identity
- bank or financial account creation
- signatures
- regulated decisions
- ethical judgment
- unusual negotiations
- physical actions
- high-risk approvals
- access controls
- ambiguous situations

This creates a second possible role for humans:

**human-as-capability.**

An agentic workflow may request a human action in the same way it requests a software tool.

## Economic metabolism

A mature agentic company should not require its human owner to continuously subsidize every API call.

A healthier target state is:

Initial capital  
→ Agentic company operates  
→ Company sells a product/service  
→ Revenue enters company  
→ Company pays operating costs  
→ Company reinvests or distributes profit

The company begins to have an economic metabolism:

**earn → spend → reinvest → distribute**

## Economic agency

Avril's deeper thesis is not automation for its own sake.

It is preserving and expanding **human economic agency** as labor becomes more automatable.
