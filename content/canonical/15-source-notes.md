## Fundraising northstar

Two founder/investor transcripts were used as strategic constraints:

### YC fundraising principles
Key ideas used:

- build a small working product before fundraising
- get users
- create leverage through motion
- convince rather than impress
- explain the business in simple language
- early rejection does not invalidate the company

### AI fundraising in 2026
Key ideas used:

- current funding is concentrated in AI infrastructure and AI applications
- enterprise AI infrastructure opportunities include cost, deployment, security, and governance
- a broad product can start with a narrow GTM
- investors care about real production usage rather than fake pilots
- important signals include customer expansion, usage, pipeline, and momentum
- early-stage pitch logic: why now, why you, differentiation
